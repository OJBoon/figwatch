# Joybuy Tone of Voice Guide — UK (United Kingdom)

## Who We Are

Joybuy is a friendly, value-driven e-commerce shopping app for the UK market. Our voice should feel warm, plain-spoken and genuinely helpful — British shoppers are quick to distrust anything that sounds like a hard sell. We are upbeat but understated: confident about the value on offer, never shouty about it.

## Tone Principles

- **Restraint over hype** — British readers respond to understatement. Let the offer speak for itself; skip "amazing", "incredible", "unbeatable" and exclamation-heavy copy.
- **Plain English** — short, everyday words over jargon or marketing-speak. If a sentence can be shorter, make it shorter.
- **Warm but not matey** — friendly and human, not over-familiar. Address the reader as "you"; avoid slang and forced chumminess ("hey guys", "grab yours").
- **Honest & specific** — lead with concrete facts (the actual saving, the actual timeframe). Vague superlatives read as empty.
- **British, not American** — UK spelling, UK terms and UK conventions throughout (see below).

## Currency & Formatting

- GBP (£) with a full stop as the decimal separator: £2.00 (not £2,00)
- Symbol before the amount, no space: £2.00
- Whole amounts may drop the decimals in body copy (£2 off), but keep them in prices and at checkout (£2.00)
- Dates: DD/MM/YYYY, or "5 July" / "5 Jul" — never MM/DD
- Quotes: single quotes for everyday use ('example'); double quotes for a quotation within
- Exclamation marks: one per screen at most — restraint is the default
- Sentence case for buttons and headings ("Shop now", not "Shop Now")

## Things to Avoid

| Avoid | Use Instead |
|---|---|
| Amazing! / Incredible! / Unbeatable! | Great value / Worth a look (measured, factual) |
| US spelling (color, favorite, personalize, catalog) | UK spelling (colour, favourite, personalise, catalogue) |
| Cart / Shipping / Zip code | Basket / Delivery (or Postage) / Postcode |
| Coupon | Voucher |
| Hey guys / grab yours / don't miss out! | Plain, direct phrasing ("you", "here's what you get") |
| Multiple or decorative exclamation marks | Zero or one per screen |
| $ or €, or £2,00 | £ with a full stop: £2.00 |
